Three Mornings of Intercession

Three mornings of prayer for someone you carry in your heart. Begin with the First.

How it works

Choose one person: a child, your husband, your mother, someone who needs God right now.

Pray one morning at a time, in order: the First, then the Second, then the Third.

When the video asks you, say that person's name out loud. The name stays between you and God: the app never asks for it or saves it.

Finished the Third? To pray for someone else, start again with the First.
