How to use your materials

  1. 1Start with the Getting Started Guide. Read it before your first early morning.
  2. 2Pray one prayer a day, in order: from Day 1 to Day 7.
  3. 3Missed a day? Pick up the next day, right where you left off. Never pray two on the same day.

When you tap “Open the PDF,” the material opens on your phone. If your phone downloads it instead, tap the notice that appears on the screen to open it.
